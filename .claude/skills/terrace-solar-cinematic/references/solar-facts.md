# Terrace Solar source facts

These are conservative notes from the supplied `Craftstrom Presentation 2026` brochure. They are source claims, not independent verification.

## Suitable for public drafting with attribution controls

- The brochure uses “semi-flexible” for one panel option.
- It describes the panels as “ultra thin” and “flexible.”
- It depicts flexible panels with an aluminum frame and stands for easy installation.
- Solar kits are described as modular in 400 W steps.
- Listed system sizes are 400 W, 800 W, 1200 W, 1600 W, and 2000 W.
- A diagram presents a 400 W kit as two 200 W panels, one microinverter, and necessary wiring.
- The brochure describes an ultra-thick ETFE layer and a proprietary material mix.
- It depicts a flat balcony mount and an adjustable 20 to 30 degree balcony/fence mount for glass panels.
- It describes a power meter that measures utility draw and throttles solar production for zero-export behavior.
- It lists a 0.1-second reaction-time claim for energy balancing.
- Battery descriptions include LiFePO4 cells, BMS, MPPT in each battery, aluminum housing, and no cobalt.
- Diagrams label a battery unit as 1 kWh and 400 W, with a 600 W DC output and 1200 W peak claim.

## Owner positioning that needs careful wording

- “Lightweight” is a desired Terrace Solar message. The brochure provides no numeric panel weight. Communicate it primarily through imagery and handling until substantiated.
- Easy installation is brochure language, but actual installation difficulty and legality depend on site and jurisdiction.

## Do not publish without current substantiation

- Certification or standards compliance.
- Safety guarantees.
- Battery cycle life, service life, charge retention, or runtime examples.
- Panel testing procedures or longevity claims.
- Universal zero-export legality or performance.
- “No permits,” universal plug-and-play, or installation-time claims.
- Prices, financing, shipping speed, availability, efficiency, dimensions, weight, warranty, savings, and returns.

When a project includes `assets/products/products.json`, use it as the detailed ledger and preserve every `unknown` value.
