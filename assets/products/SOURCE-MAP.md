## Discrepancies resolved this pass

**Mount angle.** The ledger said 20–30°. Brochure p.5 reads **"Adjustable angle 20-40 deg."**
Ledger corrected to 20–40. Note it is shown with framed glass panels, not the semi-flexible ones.

**Panel wattage.** Previously logged as a p.2 vs p.8 conflict. It is not a conflict — **both
ratings are real products.** p.13 shows a 400 W panel and a 200 W panel together making 600 W;
p.8 defines the 400 W *kit* as 2 × 200 W panels. The site now states panel ratings and system
sizes as separate figures and never conflates them.

## Still unresolved
