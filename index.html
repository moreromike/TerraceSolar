What it is

Solar that works where rooftop solar can't.

Plug-in solar is a small solar system that hangs on a railing or window ledge and plugs into a regular outdoor outlet. No roof work, and it moves with you.

Renters

Nothing is wired into the building. When you move, unplug it and take it with you.

Condo and townhome owners

No shared roof needed. A sunny balcony, patio or window ledge is enough.

HOA communities

One option your board has already reviewed, so every system looks and works the same.

The equipment

Four parts. One simple system.

Every kit comes as a matched set from one maker, so the parts are built and tested to work together.

Panels

Light enough to lift with one hand.

CraftStrom Featherweight panels are thin and semi-flexible, with no heavy glass, so they're easy to carry and easy on a railing.

About 9 lbs eachUnder 1/4 inch thickUp to 1,200 W

Inverter

Turns sunlight into outlet power.

Panels make one kind of electricity. Your home runs on another. An inverter is the small box that converts it, right at the panel.

Zero-export control

Your power stays in your home.

A small smart meter watches what your home is using, and the panels make only that much. Nothing gets pushed back onto the grid. That's what "zero-export" means.

Optional battery

Save the afternoon sun for dinner time.

A battery stores midday solar your home can't use right away and supplies it in the evening, when SDG&E power costs the most. Its outlets can also run a few small devices in an outage.

      Is plug-in solar legal in California?
      Yes, starting January 1, 2027. SB 868 lets homes use certified plug-in systems up to 1,200 watts with no utility permission or fees. The current law runs through January 1, 2030, and lawmakers can extend it. Lease and HOA rules still apply, and our community program helps HOAs and property managers set simple rules residents can follow.
      Will it work in my apartment?
      If you have a balcony, patio, yard or window ledge that gets several hours of direct sun and an outdoor outlet nearby, likely yes. South and west facing spots work best. Our free bill review will tell you for sure.
      Do I need my landlord's or HOA's permission?
      Usually it's a quick OK. Your lease and HOA rules still apply, so we make it easy. If your board has already reviewed our equipment, approval is usually quicker. If not, point your board or property manager to our community program and we'll work with them directly.
      I'm on an HOA board. How does the community program work?
      We meet with your board or management team and walk through the equipment: how it works, how it mounts and comes off, how it's designed to protect the property, and its safety certifications. We help you put simple requirements in writing, then give residents one option you've already reviewed. There's no cost to the association. Request a free community assessment below.
      Does it send power to the grid?
      No. Every kit includes zero-export control. A smart meter watches your home's use, and the system only makes what your home can use right then. With Featherweight, extra midday power can go into an optional battery.
      What if I move?
      Unplug it and take it with you. Nothing is wired into the building, so it goes where you go.
      How much will I save?
      Every kilowatt-hour your home uses from your panels is one you don't buy from SDG&E. Solar your home can't use at that moment isn't sent to the grid and earns nothing, so savings depend on your sun, your shade and how much power you use during the day. Try the calculator above, or request a compatibility review for an estimate based on your actual usage.
      Is it safe?
      Kits use certified components, plug into a standard outdoor outlet, and shut off automatically if the grid goes down. Featherweight panels have no heavy glass, which matters on a railing.

      Do I need an electrician?
      Usually not. Most homes simply plug into an outdoor outlet. If yours needs a dedicated outlet added, any licensed electrician can do it, and we'll tell you exactly what to ask for. Prefer a hand with setup? Professional installation is available on request, starting at $99.
