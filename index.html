Questions

Good to know.

Can I start with a smaller system?

Yes. The smallest system is 400 W: two panels and one Hedy microinverter.

Can I add more later?

Yes. Systems grow in 400 W steps. Storage grows by adding units, up to 4 kWh per set.

Do I need a battery?

No. Solar works on its own. A battery lets you use more of what you make, after the sun goes down.

Where can the panels go?

Balconies, terraces, fences, patios and gardens. The panels are semi-flexible and sit on stands rather than being bolted to a roof.

How does it connect to my home?

Panels connect to a Hedy microinverter, and one AC cable reaches a standard receptacle on your existing wiring. A power meter helps match production to what your home is using.

Can I move the system?

The panels sit on stands and the battery plugs into an outlet, so components are not permanently fixed to the building. Moving a system is a question for your installer and your local rules.

What rules or approvals may apply?

Requirements depend on your address: your local electrical and building rules, your utility, and your building, landlord or HOA policy. Confirm these locally, and use a licensed electrician where required, before ordering.
